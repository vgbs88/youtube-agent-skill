# Channel name

Copy this to `~/.claude/youtube/channels/<channel-handle>.md`, one file per channel, and fill it in.
Every skill in this pack reads the profile of the channel you are working on. Things that are true
of every channel you run go in `_shared.md` in the same folder. Ten minutes here is worth more than
any prompt you will ever write, because on YouTube you have to say the words out loud and a script
in the wrong voice is unreadable on camera.

- **Handle:** @handle — https://www.youtube.com/@handle
- **Language:** the language and spelling the channel publishes in (US English, British English...)
- **Market:** the country the audience is in
- **Units:** money, distance, power - whatever the channel quotes
- **Tagline:** the line the channel signs off with
- **Scope:** what the channel covers, and what it never covers

## Who I am talking to

One person. Name them properly - not "creators", but "someone with under a thousand subs who has
made nine videos and cannot work out why none of them break 400 views".

## How I actually talk

Paste the transcript of your best-performing video here, or three of them. Not what you wish you
sounded like.

## Words I never use

The ones that are not mine. Be specific: "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it".

## Words I do use

Your tics. The phrases that are yours. Keep them.

## What I will not claim

Numbers I cannot show, results that are not mine, tools I have not used. Write them down so the
skills refuse them for you instead of you catching it in the edit.

## My format

Length, how a video opens, whether there is an intro, whether you swear, whether you appear on
camera, what the desk looks like.
